# Documentation conventions

- In user-facing copy, call external service connections **integrations**. Reserve
  **plugin** for the developer Plugin API, internal compatibility names, and
  historical release notes.
- As of 2026-07-30, the deployed base quota is 2 rules and 2 integrations per
  server. Paid slot allocations produce totals of 12, 38, 76, 132, and 210.
  Cross-server destination limits are 2 on free and 10 on paid plans.
- A Role Link **syncs** 100 users on Free and up to 30 million on Premium per
  individual link. On Free it may **store** up to 10,000: writes are accepted
  past the sync limit and the overflow is held (lowest user IDs sync first),
  activating on upgrade with no re-upload. Only the storage ceiling rejects a
  write. Going over the sync limit no longer disables the link — that was the
  behavior before 2026-08-11.
- Event-driven rule processing is about 10 seconds on Free and 1.5 seconds on
  Premium (Premium is therefore ~6× faster, not the 3× quoted before
  2026-08-11); background sweeps run about every 30 minutes on Free (was 10
  before 2026-08-11) and every 2 minutes on Premium. Full reconcile is
  `ceil(members / 5,000) × interval` on Free and `ceil(members / 20,000) ×
  interval` on Premium, so a 100,000-member server catches up in about 10 hours
  on Free versus about 10 minutes on Premium.
- `static/llms-full.txt` and `static/.well-known/llms.txt` are generated by
  `npm run generate:llms`. Update canonical Markdown sources and `static/llms.txt`,
  then regenerate instead of hand-editing generated files.
